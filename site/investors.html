Investor brief

Private email is sold as an island. We’re building it as something people own.

Encrypted mail has a proven market and one shape: a company that holds your mailbox, your apps and your way out. ZeroMX splits it into parts the user can replace (relay, storage, app, directory), keeps the address and the desktop mail app people already have, and sells the managed version of each part.
