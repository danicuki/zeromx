ZeroMX seals every message sent to your address before it is stored. The sealed mailbox lives in storage you choose, and only your devices hold the key. You keep your address and the mail app you already use.

Try the demo
  • On your computer: Apple Mail, Outlook, Thunderbird
  • On your phone: the ZeroMX app
  • Storage: ours, or your own

Email gave you an address nobody can take away, and a mailbox that whoever stores it can read. Messengers gave you privacy, inside someone else’s app. ZeroMX keeps the address and seals the mailbox. Whoever stores it reads nothing at all.

01 — The idea

Three steps, and nobody who stores your mail can read it.

    Keep your address and your mail app

    Your domain, your contacts and the desktop mail app you already know. Old mail is imported and sealed on your own computer.

    Storage holds sealed files only

    Ours or your own bucket: whoever hosts it has nothing to mine, train on, leak or hand over.

    Search and AI stay on your device

    Search is instant because the mail is on your device. A local MCP server lets your own agents work with it, on your machine, with your permission.

    Nothing locks you in

    Change storage, relays or app and keep your address. Export everything at any time. Keys and the relay list go in a public directory, on Polkadot from the beta, that no company can rewrite. Not even us.
