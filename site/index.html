Your inbox knows your bank, your doctor, your lawyer and your landlord. Today your email provider can read every word. With ZeroMX it reads nothing at all.

01 — The idea

Three steps, and your mailbox stops being readable.

    No new inbox on desktop

    Your filters, labels, shortcuts and the tab you already keep open. Nothing moves.

    Your provider holds ciphertext

    For mail to your domain there is no readable archive to mine, train on, leak or hand over.

    Search and AI stay yours

    A local encrypted index gives instant search. A local MCP server lets your own agents work with your mail, on your machine, with your permission.

    Built to be checked

    Open-source relay images with reproducible builds. Keys and relay measurements in a public append-only log, designed to run on Polkadot JAM.
